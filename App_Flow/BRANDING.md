## 2. Taglines & Positioning

### Primary tagline

**"The Trust Layer for Remote Hiring"**

- Use as the main hero/positioning line.
- Emphasizes: trust, remote hiring, and SkillProof as infrastructure (“layer”).

### Alternate taglines

| Tagline | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| **"The Blockchain Resume"** | Simple, memorable; candidate-focused; differentiates on credential. |
| **"Verified skills. Tamper-proof proof."** | Short form; ads, social, badges. |
| **"Prove your skills. Trust the proof."** | Candidate acquisition; CTAs. |

### Positioning statement

*SkillProof is the trust layer for remote hiring. We verify real developer skills through AI coding interviews and store proof on blockchain, so employers can hire with confidence and candidates can prove their skills with credentials that can’t be faked.*

---

## 3. Value Proposition

**For candidates**

- Prove your skills with AI-verified, blockchain-backed credentials.  
- One shareable profile; employers can verify on-chain.  
- Stand out with objective scores and tamper-proof SkillProof NFTs.

**For employers**

- Hire verified talent; reduce fake resumes and bad hires.  
- See AI interview scores and on-chain verification.  
- Faster, more reliable screening for remote roles.

## 4. Brand Voice & Tone

| Attribute | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Clear** | No jargon unless needed (e.g. “NFT,” “on-chain” with brief context). |
| **Confident** | We enable trust; we don’t overpromise. |
| **Professional** | B2B and hiring context; still approachable. |
| **Technical but accessible** | Developer-friendly; employers and non-technical hirers can follow. |
| **Trust-focused** | Emphasize verification, proof, and transparency. |

**Avoid:** Hype, vague “revolution” claims, fear-based messaging, downplaying the role of human hiring decisions.

## 10. Do’s and Don’ts

**Do**

- Use the primary tagline prominently on the main product and landing.
- Explain “SkillProof NFT” and “on-chain” simply where first used.
- Keep voice clear, confident, and trust-focused.
- Use consistent spelling “SkillProof” and defined terms (e.g. “SkillProof badge”).

**Don’t**

- Don’t use a different primary tagline without updating this doc.
- Don’t promise “no bad hires” or “replace hiring”; we support trust and verification.
- Don’t use hype or vague “revolution” language.
- Don’t alter the logo (stretch, recolor outside palette, add effects).
