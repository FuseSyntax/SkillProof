## 1. Audience Overview

SkillProof serves two primary sides of the same market:

| Audience | Who they are | Primary outcome they want |
|----------|----------------|----------------------------|
| **Candidates** | Developers and freelancers who want to prove their skills | A trusted, shareable credential that employers can verify. |
| **Employers** | Startups and companies hiring technical talent (especially remote) | Confidence in candidate skills; fewer bad hires; verifiable proof. |

Both audiences share a need for **trust** and **portable proof** in remote technical hiring. Candidates want to be believed; employers want to believe—with evidence.

## 2. Primary Audience: Candidates

### 2.1 Definition

**Candidates** are people who sell their technical skills for work: full-time developers, contract developers, and freelancers. They may be job-seeking, open to opportunities, or building a long-term reputation. They want their skills to be **verified objectively** and **credentialed in a way they can own and share**.

### 2.2 Sub-segments

| Sub-segment | Description | Typical context |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Employed developers** | Devs with a job, open to better roles or side work | Want proof on profile (LinkedIn, portfolio); may verify 1–2 key skills. |
| **Job-seeking developers** | Actively looking for a new role | Want multiple skills verified to stand out; share profile with applications. |
| **Freelancers / independents** | Gig workers, contractors, agency devs | Need to prove skills quickly to new clients; credential must be portable. |
| **Bootcamp / career-switchers** | Newer to professional dev | Need credible proof beyond “graduated from X”; often verify core stack. |

### 2.3 Goals

- Prove skills in a way employers and clients trust.
- Get a credential they **own** (e.g. in their wallet) and can **share** (link, profile).
- Stand out in applications and on platforms (LinkedIn, job boards, freelance marketplaces).
- Avoid long, opaque hiring processes when the outcome is just “pass/fail” with no portable proof.

### 2.4 Pain Points

- Resumes and self-reported skills are often discounted.
- No widely trusted, portable way to prove technical ability.
- Vendor or platform-specific certifications feel locked in or less credible.
- Fear that assessments are unfair, opaque, or not reflective of real ability.
- Time spent on assessments that don’t result in a reusable credential.

### 2.5 Motivations & Behaviors

- **Values:** Fairness, transparency, ownership of their reputation, saving time.
- **Behavior:** Will invest 30–45 minutes in an interview if the result is a **trusted, shareable credential** (SkillProof NFT + public profile).
- **Channels:** Dev communities (Twitter, Dev.to, Hashnode, Discord), LinkedIn, job boards, freelance platforms, referrals.
- **Objections:** “Is this another test that goes nowhere?” “Do employers actually care?” “Is the scoring fair?” — Addressed by transparent scoring, on-chain verification, and “employers can verify anywhere.”

### 2.6 How SkillProof Serves Candidates

- **Objective score** from an AI coding interview (e.g. React: 78/100).
- **SkillProof NFT** they own; portable, tamper-proof credential.
- **Public profile** with shareable link; employers can verify on-chain.
- **Transparent criteria** so they understand how they’re evaluated.
- Optional **practice mode** (post-MVP) to build confidence before verification.

## 3. Primary Audience: Employers

### 3.1 Definition

**Employers** are organizations (and the people inside them) who hire technical talent: startups, SMBs, and companies. They care about **hiring the right person** and **avoiding bad hires**. They want to **trust** what they see—and ideally **verify it themselves** (e.g. on-chain), not rely on a single vendor’s word.

### 3.2 Sub-segments

| Sub-segment | Description | Typical context |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Startups (seed to Series B)** | Small tech teams, often remote-first | Need to move fast; can’t afford wrong hires; want proof without heavy process. |
| **SMBs (tech-enabled)** | Non-tech companies with dev teams | Less in-house hiring expertise; want a simple way to trust technical candidates. |
| **Companies (broader)** | Mid-size and larger orgs hiring devs | May use ATS; want verified candidates and a way to check credentials. |
| **Hiring managers / tech leads** | Individual decision-makers | Care about skill level and fit; want to see evidence (score, report, on-chain verification). |

### 3.3 Goals

- Hire developers they can trust—especially for **remote** roles.
- Reduce **bad hires** (cost, time, team impact).
- **Verify** that a candidate’s claimed skills are real, without long, opaque processes.
- Get **evidence** they can keep or share (e.g. report, on-chain check)—not only a score behind a paywall.

### 3.4 Pain Points

- Resumes and self-reported skills are unreliable; have been burned by bad hires.
- No fast, trusted way to verify remote candidates.
- Some tools offer coding tests but results stay inside that platform; no portable, verifiable credential.
- Worry about bias or opacity in automated assessments; want to understand how scores are derived.
- Time and cost of manual technical screening.

### 3.5 Motivations & Behaviors

- **Values:** Speed, trust, evidence, cost of wrong hire.
- **Behavior:** Will pay for access to verified candidates and reports if they can **verify the credential themselves** (on-chain) and see clear, explainable scores.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, job boards, referrals, hiring events, HR/recruiting tools, tech communities.
- **Objections:** “Do candidates actually use this?” “Is the AI scoring reliable?” “Why blockchain?” — Addressed by: growing verified candidate base, transparent scoring, blockchain = portable + tamper-proof proof they can verify.

### 3.6 How SkillProof Serves Employers

- **Search and filter** verified candidates by skill and score.
- **View profile** with verified skills, scores, and SkillProof NFT badges.
- **Verify on-chain** that the credential is real and owned by the candidate.
- **View / download AI interview report** (by plan) for deeper insight.
- **Trust layer** that plugs into their process; they keep their ATS and workflow.

## 6. Where They Are in the Journey

### Candidates

| Stage | Need from us |
|-------|----------------|
| **Awareness** | “There’s a way to get verified skills and a credential I own.” |
| **Consideration** | “SkillProof is fair, transparent, and the credential is verifiable by anyone.” |
| **Conversion** | Sign up, complete profile, take interview, mint NFT, share profile. |
| **Retention** | Verify more skills; update profile; refer others. |

### Employers

| Stage | Need from us |
|-------|----------------|
| **Awareness** | “There’s a pool of candidates with verified, on-chain credentials.” |
| **Consideration** | “I can verify every credential myself; I get reports and search/filter.” |
| **Conversion** | Sign up, choose plan, search, view candidates, verify on-chain. |
| **Retention** | Saved searches, alerts, repeat hires; expand usage. |
