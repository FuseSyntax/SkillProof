# SkillProof — Product Positioning

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Product Manager  
**Last Updated:** February 2025

---

## Purpose of This Document

Product positioning defines **where SkillProof sits in the market**, **who it is for**, **what problem it solves**, and **why it wins vs. alternatives**. Use this for messaging, sales, marketing, and product decisions so the product is perceived consistently.

---

## 1. Market Category

**Primary category:** AI + Blockchain Hiring Trust Platform  

**Category frame:** We are not “another coding test” or “another ATS.” We are the **trust layer** that sits between candidate claims and employer decisions: we verify skills with AI and anchor proof on blockchain so credentials are portable and tamper-proof.

**Adjacent categories (how we get described):**
- Technical skill verification
- Credential / certification platform (blockchain-backed)
- AI-powered hiring tech
- Remote hiring infrastructure

**Investor narrative (market):** Large, growing market at the intersection of AI and hiring; SaaS monetization; blockchain as trust moat; global scalability; clear “future of work” story.

---

## 2. Target Segments

### Primary: Candidates

| Segment | Description | Job to be done |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Developers** | Full-time and contract developers (all levels) | Prove skills objectively; get a shareable, trusted credential. |
| **Freelancers** | Independent devs, gig workers | Stand out to clients; prove skills without long hiring processes. |

**Characteristics:** Care about fairness, transparency, and portability of credentials. Will take a 30–45 min interview if the outcome is trusted and shareable.

### Primary: Employers

| Segment | Description | Job to be done |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Startups / SMBs** | Tech companies hiring remotely | Reduce bad hires; trust remote candidates without heavy process. |
| **Companies (broader)** | Any org hiring technical roles | Screen for real skills; verify credentials instantly. |

**Characteristics:** Care about speed, trust, and cost of wrong hires. Want proof they can verify (e.g. on-chain), not just a score behind a paywall.

### Secondary (later phases)

- **Enterprises** — High volume, ATS integration, compliance.
- **Bootcamps / educators** — Verify graduates; issue credentials.
- **Marketplaces** — Use SkillProof as verification layer for freelancer profiles.

---

## 3. Problem (Customer POV)

**Candidates**
- “Employers don’t trust my resume or self-reported skills.”
- “I have no portable, verifiable way to prove what I can do.”
- “Certifications are either not trusted or not technical enough.”

**Employers**
- “Resumes are unreliable; we’ve been burned by bad hires.”
- “We have no fast, trusted way to verify remote candidates.”
- “Coding tests exist but results stay inside one platform; we want proof we can verify ourselves.”

**Underlying theme:** Lack of **trust** and **portable proof** in remote technical hiring.

---

## 4. Our Solution (Positioning Summary)

SkillProof is the **trust layer for remote hiring**:

1. **AI** runs coding interviews and scores skills objectively.
2. **Scores** are normalized and explained (transparent criteria).
3. **Verified skills** become **SkillProof NFTs** — blockchain credentials that are tamper-proof and portable.
4. **Employers** can discover verified candidates and **verify every credential on-chain**.

**Product concept:** LinkedIn + GitHub + AI Interviewer + Blockchain Credential.

---

## 5. Positioning Statement

**Classic format:**

*For **employers and developers** who need **trust and proof in remote technical hiring**, **SkillProof** is the **trust layer** that **verifies real skills with AI and stores proof on blockchain**. Unlike **one-off coding tests or locked-in assessments**, we give **portable, on-chain credentials** that **candidates own and employers can verify anywhere**.*

**Short form:**  
*SkillProof is the trust layer for remote hiring: AI-verified skills, blockchain-backed proof.*

## 6. Key Differentiators

| Differentiator | What it means | Why it matters |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Blockchain-backed credential** | SkillProof NFT (ERC-721); candidate owns it; employer verifies on-chain. | Credential is portable and tamper-proof; not locked inside one vendor. |
| **Transparent scoring** | Explainable scoring; show how scores are calculated. | Builds trust with candidates and employers; reduces “black box” concern. |
| **AI + verification + credential** | One flow: interview → score → mint → share. | Candidate gets both assessment and a lasting credential; employer gets proof. |
| **On-chain verification** | Employers can verify NFT ownership and metadata themselves. | Trust is cryptographic; no need to “trust the platform” alone. |
| **Real-time / immediate** | Live interview flow; NFT minting right after pass. | Fast feedback and instant, shareable proof. |
| **Open verification standard (roadmap)** | Other platforms could issue SkillProof NFTs. | Network effects; potential industry standard. |

## 7. Competitive Landscape

| Type | Examples | How we’re different |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| **Coding assessments** | HackerRank, Codility | We add a **portable, blockchain credential** and **on-chain verification**; not just a score inside their product. |
| **Technical hiring platforms** | TrueUp, Triplebyte | We focus on **verification + credential**, not only matching; credential is **candidate-owned and verifiable by any employer**. |
| **Status quo** | Resumes, LinkedIn, manual interviews | We provide **objective AI assessment** and **tamper-proof proof** instead of self-report and trust. |
| **Certifications** | Vendor certs, bootcamp certs | Ours is **skill-specific**, **AI-assessed**, and **on-chain**; not tied to a single institution. |

**Positioning vs. incumbents:** We don’t replace the whole hiring process; we add a **trust layer**. Employers can keep their ATS and workflows; we plug in as “verified skills + verifiable credentials.”

---

## 8. What We Are NOT (Anti-Positioning)

- **Not an ATS** — We verify skills and issue credentials; we don’t own the full hiring pipeline.
- **Not “just another coding test”** — The outcome is a portable, on-chain credential, not only a score.
- **Not a replacement for human hiring** — We support trust and verification; humans still decide.
- **Not a generic credential platform** — We are focused on **technical skill verification** (AI coding interview + blockchain).
- **Not crypto-first** — Blockchain is for trust and portability; email login and simple UX come first.

Staying clear on this prevents scope creep and wrong positioning (e.g. “we replace hiring” or “we are a full ATS”).

## 9. Value Proposition by Segment

### Candidates

**Headline:** Prove your skills. Own your proof.

- Get an objective, AI-based skill score.
- Receive a SkillProof NFT you own and can share.
- One profile link; employers verify on-chain.
- Credential is portable across jobs and platforms.

### Employers

**Headline:** Hire verified talent. Verify every credential.

- Discover candidates with verified skills and scores.
- View AI interview insight and full report (by plan).
- Verify SkillProof NFT on-chain — no blind trust.
- Reduce risk of fake resumes and bad hires.

### Investors / Partners

**Headline:** The trust layer for remote hiring.

- Large market: AI + hiring; remote work is permanent.
- Moat: blockchain-backed, portable credentials.
- SaaS monetization (employer subscriptions, verification).
- Scalable globally; clear narrative for future of work.

## 10. Reasons to Believe (Proof Points)

Use these as evidence in messaging and sales:

- **Objective assessment** — AI conducts the interview; scoring criteria are transparent.
- **Tamper-proof** — Score and metadata hashed and stored on-chain; cannot be edited after mint.

- **Candidate-owned** — Credential lives in the candidate’s wallet; not locked in a single platform.
